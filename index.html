About me

Greetings! I am currently pursuing my PhD degree at the Machine Intelligence Laboratory (MIL) under the supervision of Prof. Tatsuya Harada.

Before that, I obtained my Master’s degree from the Intelligent Systems and Informatics (ISI) Laboratory at the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, University of Tokyo, where I was supervised by Prof. Kohei Nakajima and Prof. Yasuo Kuniyoshi. I received my Bachelor degree of Engineering in University of Waterloo, ON, Canada.

My research interests span 3D Computer Vision, Robotics, and Computational Imaging. My research follows a path From Photons to Action: understanding how sensors capture the world, interpreting its structure, and turning that understanding into robotic action.
